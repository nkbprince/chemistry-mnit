About the Department

The Department of Chemistry at MNIT Jaipur was established in 1963 and plays a key role in teaching chemical sciences to engineering students and conducting advanced research in modern interdisciplinary areas.

The department offers postgraduate and doctoral programs and contributes significantly to research relevant to technology, sustainability, and society.
